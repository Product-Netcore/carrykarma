Who is behind this

One person.
One reason.

I'm Deepesh Lakhera, a product manager in Noida. I built CarryKarma because I had a bag of clothes, a drawer of dead phones and my kids' outgrown shoes — and no single place told me where they should go. It didn't exist.

The name says the whole thing. carry — the small daily act of taking your own bag. Karma — what accumulates, for us and for our children, when we don't. If you run a collection service, work with an RWA, or just want to help, write to me. I answer every mail.

Community-sourced, not yet verified the way the NCR listings are. Handles and links only — check each before sending anything.

👇 Couple of things to know

  1. Before approaching your city's specialised recyclers, please first try and give whatever possible to your local raddiwalas — they might need your dry waste more.
  2. Many accept waste couriered from anywhere in India, provided it's clean and dry — contact the one closest to you to confirm, and send it in the condition you'd want to receive it.
  3. Collectors sometimes pause intake because of excess accumulation. Please respect that and try another on the list.
